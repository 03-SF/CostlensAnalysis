# Software Quality Engineering assignment

## Part A - Development

Design an application (web) for cost analysis.

1. Enter cost data or upload an Excel/CSV file.
2. Automatically calculate estimated cost, actual cost, variance and percentage variance.
3. Create at least two charts for comparing costs.
4. Incorporate one AI feature, such as a cost analysis summary or unusual-expense detection.
5. Generate a downloadable report containing the results.

## Part B - Cybersecurity Analysis

6. Identify at least five relevant cyber threats.
7. Explain a realistic attack scenario for each threat.
8. Propose a suitable mitigation for each attack.
9. Identify the three most harmful threats and justify the selection.
10. Design a pre-deployment security checklist.
11. Demonstrate at least three safe-data security tests, such as invalid uploads, unauthorized project access or excessive requests.

## Part C - Deployment and Reflection

12. Deploy the prototype, or demonstrate it locally if online deployment is not feasible.
13. Explain implemented controls and future work.
14. Discuss how AI improves the Excel workflow and what limitations remain.

## Expected submission

- Source code.
- Working prototype or demonstration.
- Screenshots.
- Cybersecurity threat-and-mitigation table.
- Short report documenting design, tests and limitations.

The student supplied this complete requirement list. No deadline, report word/page limit or portal upload-size limit was specified.
